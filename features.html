What VeilAI Actually Does

Most tools tell you after your photo has been misused. VeilAI stops it from being usable in the first place - here's how.

verified Peer-Reviewed Research

Face Cloaking - Invisible to People, Unreadable to AI

Before your photo is posted, VeilAI adds an imperceptible layer of adversarial noise across your face. To the human eye, nothing changes - the photo looks exactly like the original.

But to any AI model trying to learn or recognize that face, the pattern is confusing enough to make the attempt fail. That means if someone scrapes the photo to build a deepfake, train a facial-recognition tracker, or create a fake profile using your likeness - it doesn't work.

Privacy Mirror - Catching What You Might Miss

Face cloaking protects against AI misuse, but photos often carry other personal information without you noticing - an ID card in the background, a visible phone number, a license plate, or a location clue.

Privacy Mirror scans every uploaded photo for exactly this kind of visible personal detail. If anything is found, it's highlighted directly on the image, and you can blur it with a single tap before posting.
